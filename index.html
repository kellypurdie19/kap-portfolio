About Me

Hi, I'm Kelly. I've been a graphic and web designer for years, and I love taking a site that feels messy or half-done and turning it into something clean, calm, and easy to use.

I work with small business owners, coaches, and creatives who know their website should be doing more for them but don't have the time to figure it out. You bring the ideas; I bring the structure, the design eye, and the patience to get it finished.

I'm also a mom of four, so I know what it's like to juggle a lot. I keep things simple, organized, and on schedule.
