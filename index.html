About

My brain is busy. My work is calm.

I've been a graphic and web designer for years, and I love taking a site that feels messy or half-done and turning it into something clean and easy to use.

I work with small business owners, coaches, collectors, and creatives who know their website should be doing more for them. You bring the ideas; I bring the structure, the design eye, and the patience to get it finished. And as a mom of four, I keep things simple, organized, and on schedule.
